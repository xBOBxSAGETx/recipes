Method · Cold-Butter

Build & Bake

Method no creaming — the cold-butter smash is the whole game

  1. Toast: all 280g pistachios on a sheet, 325°F, 8–10 min, shake halfway, pull fragrant (not dark). Cool completely, then grind 140g in a food processor (pulse 8–10×, coarse sand); rough-chop the other 140g.
  2. Dry: whisk flour, ground pistachios, baking powder, baking soda, salt. Set aside.
  3. Butter + sugar: paddle, cold cubed butter + brown sugar + molasses. Speed 2 only, 45–60 sec. It stays chunky and uneven — that's correct. Do NOT cream to light/fluffy. Scrape the bowl.
  4. Eggs: speed 2. Add whole eggs, yolks, vanilla. Mix ~30 sec, just combined — no egg streaks. Scrape.
  5. Combine: speed 2 (stir). Dry mix in two additions: first half ~10 sec, second half stop when a few flour streaks remain. Don't walk away — overmix = tough.
  6. Fold: off the mixer. Add chocolate + chopped pistachios. Stiff spatula — cut the center, scrape the bottom, fold ~10–15 times until evenly distributed.
  7. Portion: zero a plate on the scale, scoop to 120g. Press into a rough ball with both hands — don't compress it smooth. Craggy surface cracks better. 4 per parchment tray.
  8. Ferment: wrap the tray tightly, fridge 24–36 hr (24 min, 36 ideal). The dough darkens as sugar breaks down — that's the toffee/caramel development.
  9. Bake: temper 10–15 min at room temp. 350°F, parchment, light-colored sheet, 8–10cm spacing, center rack. Rotate at 8 min. Total 14–16 min: edges golden & cracked, center jiggles when shaken. Pull at 175°F internal — carryover finishes it.
  10. Finish: flaky salt immediately. Press extra chocolate/pistachio into the surface while tacky. Cool on the pan 15 min (still baking on residual heat), then to a wire rack.
The cold-butter smash — the most important step: do NOT cream. Cold cubed butter smashed with sugar on low speed keeps air out. Air = cakey; no air = dense, fudgy, chewy. Creaming to light-and-fluffy (or mixing above speed 2) is the #1 way this cookie goes wrong.

Troubleshooting

Flat / spreadUnder-fermented or butter too warm → re-chill; butter must be cold cubed
CakeyCreamed too long / above speed 2 → cold cubed butter, speed 2, 45–60 sec max
Airy / crumblyToo much baking soda → verify 3g, not more
ToughOvermixed at combine → pull at 14 min; check internal temp
Raw centerBalls too cold → temper 10–15 min, or add 2 min bake
Edges brown, center rawOven too hot for ball size → drop to 325°F, extend 2–3 min
Too sticky to portionDough too warm → fridge 15 min, then portion
BlandUnder-salted → verify 6g salt + the flaky finish

Notes the non-obvious bits

Chocolate: eating-quality bar, hand-chopped ~1cm — not chips (stabilizers block melt pools).

Pistachios: cool completely before grinding or they oil out to paste.

Make ahead: freeze portioned balls solid, bag; bake from frozen +2–3 min (keeps 3 months).

Storage: airtight, room temp, 4–5 days — never refrigerate (staling).
