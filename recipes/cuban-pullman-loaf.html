Cuban Pullman Sandwich Loaf

Cuban-bread base → tangzhong for a soft, tight crumb → lidded pain-de-mie bake · one 13×4″ loaf
Pan
13×4×4″ lidded
Dough
~948 g
Flour
Sir Lancelot 14.2%
Hydration
66%
Bake
375°F lidded
What changed from the Cuban base: same lard/sugar/salt ratios; hydration 63% → 66% plus a tangzhong (a flour-water roux from within the formula) that keeps high-gluten flour soft. The lid gives a thin crust + tight square crumb — no steam, no score.

Formula Baker's % on total flour (incl. the tangzhong flour)

IngredientBaker %Weight

Sugar2%11 g

Tangzhong (cook first, taken FROM the formula above): 25 g flour + 125 g water. The dough then gets the remaining 515 g flour and 231 g water plus everything else. Don't add these as extra — it's a split, not an addition.

Method KitchenAid + folds · ~4–4.5 h at 70°F · grease pan AND lid

  1. Tangzhong: whisk 25 g flour + 125 g water in a small pot. Cook on low, stirring, until it thickens to a paste and the whisk leaves lines (~65°C), ~2–3 min. Scrape out, cool to warm.
  2. Bloom ADY: yeast + the sugar in ~90 g of the water at 100–110°F, 5–10 min to foam. Over ~115°F kills it.
  3. Mix to incorporate, then bench rest: everything in — remaining flour (515 g), tangzhong, bloom, the rest of the water, salt, lard. KitchenAid low just until combined (no dry bits), then stop — the hook can't windowpane this flour (high-gluten, same as your bagels). Turn out and rest 20–30 min on the granite — a bench rest, not a true autolyse. Dough 76–78°F.
  4. Develop by stretch-and-fold: 2–3 S&F sets ~30 min apart through the first ~1.5 h of bulk — the folds build to windowpane (a post-fold check). Bulk on to doubled at 70°F, by the dough (granite's a heat-sink — go by the rise).
  5. Shape to the pan: grease pan and lid. Degas gently; roll a tight 13″ cylinder, seam down, to the pan length — or divide into 3 equal pieces, roll each tight, set side-by-side (the evenest crumb). Settle seam-down.
  6. Proof in the pan to ~1″ below the rim (~80–85% full) at 70°F, ~45–75 min. Slide the lid on. Don't over-proof — lidded + over-proof squashes the crumb or blows the lid.
  7. Bake 375°F, lidded the whole time, ~35–40 min to 200–205°F internal. The lid traps steam — flat top, thin soft crust. (Peek near the end; don't unlid early or the top dries.)
  8. Turn out immediately onto a rack; optional butter/lard brush on top for a softer crust. Cool COMPLETELY before slicing — warm sandwich bread gums and tears. Serrated knife.

Read & Fix Soft, tight, sliceable — cause → correction

Dense, squat crumbUnder-developed or under-proofed. Fold to windowpane, and proof to ~1″ under the rim before the lid goes on.
Blew the lid / squeezed out the seamsOver-proofed or too much dough. Proof a touch less; hold dough to ~948 g for this pan.
Gummy, tears when slicedCut warm, or under-baked. Cool completely and pull at 200–205°F internal.
Crust too thick / toughBaked too hot/long, or unlidded. Keep the lid on at 375°F, pull at temp — don't bake to color. Once cool, wrap airtight (never fridge it).
