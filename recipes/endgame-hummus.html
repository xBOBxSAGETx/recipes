Endgame Hummus

Skinned · whipped · ice-finished · restaurant-smooth at home
Yield
~750 g
Active
15 min + skinning
Tahini Ratio
110 g / 480 g peas
Process
8–10 min

Ingredients skin the chickpeas — it’s the difference between grainy and silk

Chickpeas drained & skinned, 2 cans480 g
Tahini110 g
Lemon juice ~1 lemon35 g
Garlic oil54 g
Ice cubes78 g
Fine sea salt6 g
Cumin1 g
Fried garlic bits to finish—

Method the order is the technique — don’t reshuffle it

  1. Whip tahini: process the tahini alone for 90 sec until lightened and fluffy — pale and aerated.
  2. Add lemon: processor running, stream in the lemon juice, process 30 sec to emulsify.
  3. Chickpeas: add half the chickpeas with salt + cumin, process 1 min; add the rest, process 2 min to a smooth paste.
  4. Ice finish: drop in the ice cubes, run 4–5 min — the cold + extended processing whips it to a mousse.
  5. Garlic oil: stream it in with the processor running until glossy and unified.
  6. Season: adjust lemon + salt, pushing it slightly over — flavor mutes in the fridge.
  7. Serve: swoosh into a bowl with the back of a spoon, pool garlic oil in the crater, scatter fried garlic bits.
Three moves make it endgame. Skinning the chickpeas removes the only source of grit. Whipping the tahini solo first aerates the base before anything weighs it down. The ice finish is the secret — it keeps the mass cold through 4–5 min of processing that would otherwise heat and split it, building a mousse-light texture you can’t get any other way. Season a touch heavy; the chill rounds it off.
