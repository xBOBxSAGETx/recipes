The spread: cabbage, radish, onion, oregano, lime.

Two non-negotiables: collagen + simmer for body — the feet/neck and a lazy 190–200°F simmer for 2.5–3 hr are what make the broth rich, not thin; and frying the chile paste to brick/burgundy in shimmering lard, which cooks out the raw edge. Bloom the drained hominy in that hot paste off-heat 15–20 min so it drinks the chile-lard.

Broth · Paste · Finish

Build the Pot

Method broth 2.5–3 hr · start the paste at the 1-hour mark

  1. Broil: onions quartered + garlic halved, cut-side up, 6–8″ from the broiler, 8–12 min to char (500°F+). Reserve 1 onion for the paste.
  2. Prep: debone the shoulder, cube 2–3″, reserve the bone. Split the feet lengthwise if using.
  3. Brown: shimmering lard, brown the pork in 3–4 batches, 2–3 min/side, to build fond (400–450°F).
  4. Simmer: add bone, collagen cuts, pork, aromatics, bay, water. Lazy bubbles 2.5–3 hr at 190–200°F. Skim at 30 min; start the paste at 1 hr.
  5. Toast: dry skillet — árbol 15–20 sec/side, guajillo/ancho/pasilla 30–60 sec/side. Watch for the puff, then stem + seed.
  6. Soak: off-boil hot water, submerge the chiles 25 min. Reserve the liquid.
  7. Blend: chiles + a cup of the liquid + the roasted onion + raw garlic + spices. Strain hard, rinsing with a little broth.
  8. Fry: 4 spoons of lard, shimmering. Add the paste (it sizzles hard), vinegar at 30 sec, stir 3–4 min to brick/burgundy (375–400°F).
  9. Bloom: kill the heat, stir in the drained hominy, rest 15–20 min off heat to absorb the chile-lard.
  10. Finish: pork at 195–205°F → remove. Strain + skim the broth, add the chile-hominy, simmer 15 min, return the pork 5 min. Salt; lime if flat.
  11. Serve: ladle 70% broth, 30% solids; tear the pork by hand into irregular chunks. Pass the garnishes.
