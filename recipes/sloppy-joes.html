No-water method: the 315 g of onion + pepper is the liquid — they sweat their moisture into the glaze, so it never tastes dilute. Retain 30–45 g of the rendered fat after browning (drain the rest): too much reads greasy, too little and the bloom/sweat goes dry. Method · The Glaze

Sear, Bloom & Glaze

Method ~25 min — sear hard, bloom the paste, reduce to a glaze

  1. Season: toss 907 g beef with 8 g salt + 2 g coarse pepper. Mix gently — don’t compact it.
  2. Sear: hot skillet, add beef, break into chunks, brown well to build fond (375–400°F). Retain 30–45 g fat, drain the excess.
  3. Sweat: add onion + pepper, cook 5–6 min until softened and the moisture releases. Add garlic the last 1–2 min only (prevents bitter oxidation).
  4. Bloom: clear the center, add tomato paste + guajillo + smoked paprika, stir in the center 60 sec until the paste darkens (325–350°F).
  5. Build: add ketchup, Worcestershire, mustard, brown sugar, and 10 g ACV (reserve 5 g). Stir to combine.
  6. Reduce: simmer uncovered 10–15 min. Test: drag a spoon through — the channel should hold 3–5 sec before closing.
  7. Finish: off heat, add the reserved 5 g ACV + 1 g fine pepper. Taste, adjust salt, cover and rest 5 min.

Troubleshooting salt window: ~8 g on beef, sauce adds ~4–5 g — taste-add only at the end
