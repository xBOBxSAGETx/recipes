Stage 1  Nixtamalization Cook + overnight steep — the chemistry happens here

  1. Rinse: rinse corn under cold water; remove debris and broken kernels.
  2. Dissolve: stir 7 g cal into 2 L water — it goes milky/cloudy. Fully dissolved before the corn goes in.
  3. Cook: add corn, bring to a boil (~10 min), then gentle simmer 15–25 min. Done when the pericarp (skin) slips off easily when rubbed and the kernel is soft.
  4. Steep: off the heat, covered, 8–12 hr (overnight ideal) — the cal keeps penetrating.
  5. Wash: drain, then rinse 3–5×, rubbing to shed the loosened skins, until the water runs mostly clear. Keep it damp — do not dry. Yields ~1,500 g wet nixtamal.

Stage 2 · Stage 3

Grind, Press & Cook

Stage 2  Grind & Hydrate ~1,400 g masa — Play-Doh texture is the goal

  1. Load: the grinder holds ~750 g wet nixtamal — load the first half, no extra water yet (inherent moisture is ~45–50%).
  2. Grind: run 10–15 min, scraping the sides every 2–3 min. A coarse paste forms from the kernel moisture alone.
  3. Hydrate: add warm water incrementally — 15–30 ml per half, 5 ml at a time, kneading between — to 55–60% moisture.
  4. Test: smush a ball flat — smooth edges, no cracks; earlobe-soft; clean release from your hands; a pea-sized ball floats.
  5. Combine: scrape out, repeat with the second half, combine, knead 2–3 min. Add a pinch of salt if using.
  6. Rest: cover with a damp towel, rest 10–20 min — hydration equalizes and flavor develops.

Stage 3  Press & Cook Three flips — the third is the puff

  1. Portion: weigh 32 g balls (~40–45 total), roll smooth, keep covered.
  2. Press: ball between parchment/plastic — press once, rotate 180°, press again for even ~1.5 mm thickness, no thin spots.
  3. First side: onto the preheated dry comal; cook until the edges dry and lift slightly (~15 sec).
  4. Flip 1: cook until the color changes and light spots appear across the surface (~40 sec).
  5. Flip 2 (puff): flip back to the first side — it should puff; a gentle press on the edges with a towel helps (10–15 sec).
  6. Stack: transfer to a towel-lined container, cover immediately — the steam keeps them pliable.

Quality · Storage

Dial It In & Keep

Quality Targets All five trace back to hydration or comal heat

TestWhat you wantIf not
Smush testEdges smooth, no cracksAdd water 5 ml at a time
Earlobe testSame softness as an earlobeToo dry: add water. Too wet: add masa.
Float testPea-sized ball floatsUnder-hydrated — knead in more water
Crumple testUnfurls without crackingOvercooked or under-hydrated
PuffFull inflation on the third flipComal temp, thickness, or hydration is off
Hydration is the master variable. Four of the five tests above are really one question — is the masa at 55–60%? Adjust in 5 ml steps; it moves faster than you think. The puff is the one test that also depends on the comal: too cool and the steam can’t inflate the tortilla, too thick and it can’t lift.
