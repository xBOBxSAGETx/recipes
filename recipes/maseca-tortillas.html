Stage 1  Make the Masa Play-Doh that doesn’t crack at the edges is the whole game

  1. Toast a quarter. Dry-toast 100 g of the Maseca (no oil) in a comal/skillet over medium, stirring, until it smells nutty and just barely colors — 3–5 min. Don’t brown it (turns bitter). This puts back toasty depth that drying strips out. Stir it into the other 300 g.
  2. Hydrate. Melt the 16 g lard into the 460 g hot water. Stir the salt into the Maseca, then pour in the lard-water gradually, mixing as you go.
  3. Knead. Work 2–3 min to a smooth Play-Doh — press a ball flat: smooth edges, no cracks. Too dry → add water 5 ml at a time; too wet → dust in Maseca.
  4. Rest. Cover with a damp towel, 20–30 min — the masa fully hydrates and firms. Re-check the smush test after; it usually wants a touch more water.

Stage 2 · Press, Cook & Keep

Press & Cook

Stage 2  Press & Cook three flips — the third is the puff

  1. Portion: roll 32 g balls (~28 total), smooth, keep covered so they don’t skin over.
  2. Press: ball between plastic (a cut produce/zip bag) — press once, rotate 180°, press again to an even ~1.5 mm, no thin spots.
  3. Cook, 3 flips: dry comal at 400–450°F (IR). Side 1 ~15 sec until the edges set and lift; flip 1 ~40 sec until light spots appear; flip 2 back to side 1 — it should puff (nudge the edges with a towel).
  4. Steam-rest: stack into a towel-lined container and cover immediately — the trapped steam is what keeps them soft and foldable.
