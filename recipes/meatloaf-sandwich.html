Soy sauce15 g
Worcestershire10 g
Fish sauce5 g

Seasonings
Salt10 g
Black pepper3 g
Garlic powder12 g

Paprika4 g

  1. Base: preheat 350°F. Sauté onion + garlic in butter until soft; add tomato paste, cook 1 min, cool. Whisk eggs, milk, soy, Worcestershire, fish sauce.
  2. Bind: add breadcrumbs + all seasonings + brown sugar to the egg mix, rest 5 min, then add beef + aromatics and mix firmly to bind.
  3. Shape: line a loaf pan with plastic wrap, pack tight, flip onto a rack-on-sheet, peel the wrap. Bake 45–50 min.
  4. Glaze: coat with ketchup, bake 15 min more to 160°F internal, then rest 10 min before slicing.
Resting is non-negotiable — slice it straight out of the oven and the juices run out into a dry, crumbly mess. Ten minutes lets them redistribute. The rack-on-sheet + free-form shape (vs. baked in the pan) lets all sides brown and the fat drain instead of stewing.

Max Crisp Fries · Seasoning

The Fries

Max Crisp Fries 4 russets · soak overnight · 450°F on preheated pans

Russet potatoes4
(700–800 g)—
Rice flour8 g
Cornstarch8 g
Neutral oil split30 g
Baking soda in parboil water3 g
Salt in parboil water10 g
  1. Soak: cut ¼″ fries, soak overnight. Parboil in salted + baking-soda water 2–3 min, drain, steam-dry 5 min.
  2. Coat: preheat 450°F with two sheet pans inside. Dust the fries with rice flour + cornstarch, toss with 15 g oil, spread on the hot pans.
  3. Bake: 20 min, then flip, add the remaining oil, swap pan positions, bake 20–25 min more. Optional broil 2 min. Season immediately.
Two tricks do the crisping. The overnight soak rinses surface starch so the exterior shatters instead of gumming; the baking soda in the parboil raises pH, breaking down pectin for a fluffy interior. Preheated pans give the bottoms an instant sear — cold pans steam.

Aioli · Onions · Assembly

Build the Sandwich

A1 Aioli Doubled batch — raw + roasted garlic for depth

Mayonnaise240 g
A1 steak sauce100 g
Dijon mustard24 g
Garlic 12 g roast, 12 g raw24 g
Lemon juice10 g
Worcestershire6 g
Black pepper2 g
Cayenne / hot sauceto taste

Roast half the garlic until golden, mash both roasted and raw to a paste, whisk with everything else, chill 30 min. All raw is too sharp; all roasted too mellow — the mix is the point.

Sautéed Onions Low and slow — rushing steams them, never caramelizes

Yellow onion sliced thin150 g
Butter10 g
Olive oil10 g
Salt1 g
Black pepper0.5 g
Worcestershire / balsamic3 g

Cook in butter + oil over low heat until deep golden — 20–30 min, stirring occasionally (color is the target, not the clock). Finish with the Worcestershire or balsamic.

Arugula, pickles optional—
  1. Melt: toast the bread lightly; warm a meatloaf slice, top with cheese, melt under the broiler.
  2. Close: spread aioli on both slices, layer meatloaf + cheese, onions, and extras; close, slice, serve with the fries.
