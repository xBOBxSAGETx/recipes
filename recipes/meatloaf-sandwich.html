Meatloaf Sandwich

Free-form glazed meatloaf · max-crisp oven fries · A1 aioli · ~4 sandwiches
Yield
~4 sandwiches

~2 hr

Meatloaf 907 g beef (85/15) · free-form on a rack · 160°F internal

Seasonings
Salt10 g

Paprika4 g
Thyme1 g

  1. Base: preheat 350°F. Sauté onion + garlic in butter until soft; add tomato paste, cook 1 min, cool. Whisk eggs, milk, soy, Worcestershire, fish sauce.
  2. Bind: add breadcrumbs + all seasonings + brown sugar to the egg mix, rest 5 min, then add beef + aromatics and mix firmly to bind.
  3. Shape: line a loaf pan with plastic wrap, pack tight, flip onto a rack-on-sheet, peel the wrap. Bake 45–50 min.
  4. Glaze: coat with ketchup, bake 15 min more to 160°F internal, then rest 10 min before slicing — skip the rest and it’s dry and crumbly.

Max Crisp Fries 4 russets · soak overnight · 450°F on preheated pans

Russet potatoes 700–800 g4
Rice flour8 g
Cornstarch8 g
Neutral oil split30 g
Baking soda in parboil water3 g
Salt in parboil water10 g
  1. Soak: cut ¼″ fries, soak overnight (rinses surface starch). Parboil in salted + baking-soda water 2–3 min (baking soda raises pH for a fluffy interior), drain, steam-dry 5 min.
  2. Coat: preheat 450°F with two sheet pans inside. Dust with rice flour + cornstarch, toss with 15 g oil, spread on the hot pans.
  3. Bake: 20 min, flip, add the remaining oil, swap pan positions, bake 20–25 min more (optional broil 2 min). Season HOT.

Run of show (~2 hr): start the aioli and chill → onions on low and slow → mix, shape, and bake the meatloaf (350°F to 160°F), then rest 10–15 min → during that rest, parboil and bake the fries at 450°F → toast bread and melt cheese on the meatloaf → season fries hot, build, serve. The meatloaf rest and the overnight fry soak are the two non-negotiables.
