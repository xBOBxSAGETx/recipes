Total ~1,294g dough → eight ~160g loaves. KitchenAid handles this in one batch — a docile dough, opposite of high-gluten loads.

Method Same-day, single rise, ~4–4.5 hr at 70°F

  1. Bloom the ADY: dissolve yeast + sugar in ~100g of the water at 100–110°F. Wait 5–10 min for foam — that's proof of life. Too cold = sluggish, over ~115°F = dead.
  2. Mix: combine flour, salt, lard, the bloom, and remaining water. Knead to moderate gluten — smooth and soft, NOT full windowpane. ~8–10 min.
  3. Bulk rise: ~1.5–2 hr until doubled at 70°F. Go by the dough (doubled), not the clock — at room temp 1.5% ADY isn't as fast as a warm proofbox. (model-reasoned; first bake locks the real time)
  4. Divide & shape: 8 × 160g. Short ~8″ torpedoes with blunt rounded ends (not tapered points). Seam-side down.
  5. Proof: 45–60 min until puffy and light.
  6. Score: one straight lengthwise slash per loaf.
  7. Bake 400°F with steam (pan of water / ice cubes) first 10 min, then dry. ~20–22 min to pale golden, internal 200–205°F. Do NOT bake dark.
  8. Finish soft: brush hot loaves with melted lard or butter. Keeps the crust soft and pressable.
Lard does double duty: in the dough it shortens gluten for the soft, cottony crumb; brushed on hot it keeps the crust soft. Same lard as the pork roast — one ingredient ties the whole Cubano system together.

Why It's Built This Way

Principles & Reads

Key Principles Cuban bread breaks the high-gluten instinct

AP flour, go DOWN in proteinOpposite of pizza/bagels. ~11% AP gives a tender, soft crumb that compresses cleanly in the press. High-gluten flour bakes tough and eats rubbery here.
Lard is structural~4% shortens gluten strands — the soft, almost cottony crumb and tender bite. Without it you've made a French loaf, not Cuban bread.
Pale, thin crust by design400°F and pull at pale golden. Hard browning makes a thick chewy crust — the wrong vehicle for a press. Fights your char instinct; that's correct.
Same-day, lean & fastNo cold ferment, no poolish. Flavor comes from lard + bake, not long fermentation. High yeast, one bulk, done in hours.
Moderate gluten onlyKnead to smooth/soft, not windowpane. Over-developed gluten makes it chewy and press-resistant.

Diagnostics

Tough / chewy, fights the pressFlour too strong or over-kneaded. Use AP, stop at moderate gluten.
Thick, hard crustBaked too long / too hot / too dark. Pull paler, hold 400°F, brush with fat out of the oven.
Dense, didn't riseDead/sluggish ADY. Bloom water was too hot or too cold — confirm foam before mixing.
Crumb too open / holeyOver-proofed or hydration creeping high. Tighten the shape, watch the proof.
Crust crisp then leatherySkipped the post-bake fat brush. Brush hot for a soft, pressable crust.
Built for the press: every choice here — AP flour, lard, pale thin crust, soft finish — optimizes this loaf as the vehicle for the pressed Cubano, not as a standalone artisan loaf. Pair with Cuban Pork & Mojo v2.0.
