Grind everything together in a spice grinder to a uniform powder — whole spices ground fresh are far more potent than pre-ground (the volatile oils oxidize fast). Grind the whole spices first, then pulse the pre-ground ones (paprika, turmeric) in to homogenize. Cardamom: green pods, seeds only — the papery husk is flavorless filler; the 1.5g is seed weight. Allspice is the signature note — 9% is conservative, bump to 12–15% if timid; go easy on clove, it dominates fast. Blend keeps 6+ months sealed.

Why oil-based, not yogurt: oil carries the fat-soluble spice compounds into the meat. Yogurt forms a barrier — it seasons the coating, not the chicken. Acid held to 5–6%: thin slices absorb more, and excess acid turns the texture mushy. Never exceed 48 hr marinade — the acid degrades the protein.

Method · Sear · Troubleshoot

Cook & Finish

Part 3  Method Active ~30 min — serve immediately, no rest

  1. Slice: trim fat, slice thighs horizontally to ¼″ (6mm). Thin = complete flavor penetration — the whole reason the inside isn't bland.
  2. Build: whisk oil, lemon, garlic, salt, and spices until deeply tinted. Taste — it should read aggressive; the meat tames it.
  3. Marinate: coat every piece, bag or container, refrigerate 12–24 hr (24 = full development). Max 48 hr.
  4. Temper: pull from fridge 30 min early. Heat cast iron / flat-top griddle until screaming hot — IR-confirm 450–500°F.
  5. Sear: single-layer batches, 2–3 min/side for char. Let the surface recover between batches — crowding = steam, no char. Target 15–20% surface char.
  6. Chop: all pieces back on the hot surface, chop with two spatulas, let sit 30–60 sec, toss. Builds crispy edges.
  7. Serve: immediately, no rest — crispy edges degrade fast. Pull at 165–175°F. Taste before wrapping; it should eat great plain.
Chop-and-sear replicates the spit: a vertical shawarma spit does two things — bastes in rendered fat and chars on high heat. Thin slice + oil marinade + screaming-hot iron + the chop step reproduces both at home. Flat-top advantage: more surface = bigger single-layer batches without crowding, which is the #1 cause of no char.
