Workflow same freezer engine as the rest — keep everything cold

  1. Coarse grind: pork butt (~70/30), everything near-freezing, through the ½″ (coarse) plate.
  2. Dose: weigh the ground meat; add the seasoning at the % above, plus the blend’s liquid — breakfast: up to 14 ml/lb water (juicier, optional); chorizo: the 5% vinegar (run it — it’s what makes it chorizo).
  3. Mix: ~2 min until tacky and bound (primary bind = holds together) — keep it cold; warm fat smears and goes greasy.
  4. Regrind fine: run both blends back through the 3/16″ plate — Legg’s method seasons between the two grinds; gives the JD-smooth texture.
  5. Portion + freeze: into 450 g bags, press flat, remove air, label, freeze.
  6. Cook: thaw. Breakfast — patties / crumble on the griddle to 160°F. Chorizo — crumble and fry until rendered and crisp.
This is the premix baseline — run it, learn your grind and fat, and it becomes the bar the scratch versions have to beat. Fat is the texture: ~30% (pork butt) keeps both juicy; leaner reads dry and crumbly. Cold is non-negotiable — chill the meat, grinder parts, and bowl; smeared fat is the #1 way sausage goes greasy and won’t bind. Chorizo needs its acid — the 5% vinegar (~4%, 19 ml/lb) is what makes Blend 111 taste like chorizo. Legg’s marks it optional — run it anyway.
