Char · Build · Simmer

Roast & Build

Method char aromatics too (onion halved + garlic, 30–40% char) for a deeper upgrade

  1. Char: fire-roast all peppers over flame or under the broiler, rotating to 85–90% blackened. Steam covered in a bowl 15 min, then peel — leave 10–15% charred flecks — and stem/seed. Rough-chop half (body), leave half in big pieces (texture).
  2. Build base: heat the tallow over medium-high, add the onion and cook to deep golden with fond, 8–10 min. Add garlic + oregano + cumin, 30 sec until fragrant.
  3. Add peppers: stir them in to coat, pour in the stock, bring to a simmer.
  4. Thicken: sprinkle flour ~8 g at a time, stirring constantly, until it coats a spoon and holds when drizzled (usually 16–32 g).
  5. Chicharrón: add the rough 1″ pieces, simmer 30–45 min. Most melts in for richness; the bigger pieces stay. Done when they bend, not snap.
  6. Finish: salt aggressively — it needs more than you think. Optional 5–10 ml vinegar or lime at the end for brightness. Final texture: thick enough to sit on a burrito without running.

Troubleshooting

ProblemCauseFix
Thin / runnyUnder-thickenedFlour slurry: 8 g flour + 30 ml water
BitterOver-charred peppersPinch of sugar; can’t fully fix

No pork depthChicharrón too thinThicker cut; add extra lard
Chicharrón mushySimmered too longAdd half early, half in the last 10 min
Too thickOver-flouredThin with stock, re-adjust salt
The chicharrón is seasoning, not bulk — most dissolves into the sauce for richness; the few intact pieces you find are a bonus, so don’t over-add. Poblano sub: no chilaca? Use poblano, char harder, and add 1–2 extra serranos. Pork-forward upgrade: roast 1 kg pork neck (220°C / 425°F, 45 min), simmer in 2 L water 3–4 hr, and use that as the stock. Storage: best same or next day; the chicharrón version keeps ~48 hr — for longer, freeze the base without chicharrón (3 months) and stir it in fresh on reheat.
