Method

Rice, Prep & Roll

Method Rice first — everything else waits on it cooling to body temp

  1. Rinse: rice in a fine mesh strainer, agitate under cold water until it runs crystal clear (2–3 min).
  2. Cook: pressure cooker — 300 g rice + 300 g water (1:1 by weight), high pressure 3 min, natural release 10 min. Tender, separate grains.
  3. Season: warm vinegar + sugar + salt until dissolved. Transfer hot rice to a wide bowl, drizzle while cutting/folding (not stirring), and fan to body temp, glossy.
  4. Crab mix: shred surimi by hand into thin strips, mix with 30 g Kewpie + a pinch of salt — creamy and cohesive.
  5. Spicy mayo: combine 15 g Kewpie + 15 g sriracha; adjust toward 2:1 for hotter, 3:1 for milder.
  6. Tempura: from frozen — air fryer 400°F, 8–10 min, flip halfway (oven: 425°F, 12–15 min). Golden, crispy.
  7. Prep veg: avocado thin, cucumber into matchsticks, green onion on the bias, lemon mandolined paper-thin. Uniform cuts.
  8. Lay nori: shiny-side down on a dry surface; if humid, flash-toast over a flame ~2 sec/side so it’s crisp.
  9. Spread rice: wet hands; spread ~150 g rice over the center third, leaving a ½″ border top and bottom to seal. ~¼″ thick, even.
  10. Fill: layer across the center — tuna, crab mix, tempura shrimp, avocado, cucumber, lemon, green onion. Drizzle spicy mayo + eel sauce. Don’t overfill.
  11. Roll: fold the bottom edge over the filling and tuck tight; continue away from you keeping tension; seal the edge with a wet finger.
  12. Cut: wet a sharp knife, cut in half on the bias, wipe the blade between cuts for a clean face.

Troubleshooting Rice ratio: 300 : 300 : 60 : 25 : 6 — rice : water : vinegar : sugar : salt

ProblemCauseFix
