Chicken Wild Rice Soup

Whole bird · wild rice-forward · thick, spoon-coating, Upper Midwest style

Key Targets the cues that decide the pot

Poach waterBare simmer, 180–195°F — surface shivers, a stray bubble. A boil clouds the stock and tightens the meat.
Pull the birdThickest thigh 175–180°F, leg wiggles loose — about 50–70 min.
Wild riceMost kernels split open. Lake/hand-parched ~20–30 min; glossy black paddy ~45–60 min.
Sweat & rouxButter foaming, never browning; vegetables soft with no color. Roux 2–3 min — smells toasty, stays blond.
ThicknessCoats the back of a spoon before the rice goes in — the rice thickens it further.
Cream inLast, heated through below a boil.
Taste only after the chicken and rice are in. About 2 kg of unseasoned meat and rice goes in at the end and soaks up salt — a base that tastes right before them tastes flat after. Expect to go past the 10 g of salt; add it in small hits.

Execution

Method rice + vegetable prep run during the carcass simmer

  1. Poach: chicken, cold water, and the stock onion, carrot, celery in the pot. Bring up to a bare simmer (180–195°F), skim foam for the first 15 min. Poach until the thigh reads 175–180°F, about 50–70 min.
  2. Pull: lift the bird out; keep the pot at a bare simmer. Cool 15 min, strip all meat, discard skin, shred bite-size. Bones and cartilage back into the pot.
  3. Carcass: covered, bare simmer 60–90 min. Covered = no reduction.
  4. Rice (meanwhile): rinse, then simmer covered in the 1800 g water, salted like pasta water, until most kernels split. Drain.
  5. Strain: strain the stock, skim the fat and save it. Weigh out 2500 g for the soup (short? top up with water); keep the extra for leftovers. Dissolve the base in a ladle of the hot stock.
  6. Sweat: clean pot, butter (up to half swapped for the chicken fat) until foaming. Onion, carrot, celery until soft with no color, ~10 min.
  7. Mushrooms: cook until their liquid is gone and the edges brown, ~10 min. Garlic, 1 min.
  8. Roux: flour over the vegetables, stir 2–3 min until it smells toasty and coats everything. Stays blond.
  9. Liquid: sherry in, scrape the bottom. Whisk in the stock a ladle at a time (a dump makes lumps), then the dissolved base, thyme, bay, and 5 g of the salt. Bring to a simmer.
  10. Simmer: uncovered 20–30 min, scraping the bottom as you stir (the roux settles and scorches), until the raw-flour taste is gone and it coats a spoon.
  11. Finish: bay out. Chicken, rice, cream in; heat through below a boil. Now taste — salt in small hits, pepper, then lemon off the heat. Parsley.

Troubleshooting three dials: base · salt · lemon

ProblemCauseFix
Weak on chickenPoach stock is light by natureMore base, a little at a time — before more salt (the base is salty)
Flat overallChicken + rice absorbed the saltSalt in small hits, stir, re-taste
Heavy, dullCream + roux with no liftMore lemon, a little at a time, off the heat
Too thinShort simmer / rice not in yetSimmer longer; it sets up further as it sits
Scorched bottomRoux settled, not scrapedMove to a clean pot without scraping the burnt layer
Leftovers set solidWild rice keeps absorbing in the fridgeThin with the saved stock when reheating
