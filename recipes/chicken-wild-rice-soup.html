Chicken Wild Rice Soup

Whole bird · thick & creamy · Upper Midwest style
Yield
~6 L
Time
~2.5 hr
Pots
10 qt + 8 qt

Ingredients

Chicken & Rice
Whole chicken 4.5–5 lb2.0–2.3 kg
Water poach6000 g
Wild rice blend Floating Leaf325 g
Water rice650 g
Heavy cream430 g
Kosher salt start10 g
Black pepper4 g
Lemon juice36 g
Parsley chopped15 g
Soup Base
Unsalted butter150 g
Onion · carrot · celery diced360 · 270 · 270 g
Cremini sliced400 g
Garlic minced22 g
All-purpose flour110 g
Dry sherry55 g
Better Than Bouillon Roasted Chicken30 g
Knorr chicken bouillon powder~20 g
Dried thyme · bay leaves4 g · 2

Method

  1. Poach: chicken + 6000 g water in the 10 qt pot. Bare simmer, never a boil, until the thigh hits 175°F (~1 hr).
  2. Rice (meanwhile): 325 g rice + 650 g water. Boil, cover tight, low for 25 min, rest 5 min covered. Dry? A splash more water.
  3. Pull: bird out, meat off, skin and bones tossed. Shred. The pot liquid is your stock — keep 2500 g, save the rest.
  4. Vegetables: 8 qt pot, 150 g butter. Onion, carrot, celery until soft (~10 min). Mushrooms until browned (~10 min). Garlic, 1 min.
  5. Thicken: 110 g flour, stir 2 min. 55 g sherry. Whisk in the 2500 g stock gradually, then 30 g Better Than Bouillon, ~20 g Knorr, 4 g thyme, bay, 5 g salt. Simmer 20–30 min, stirring the bottom, until thick.
  6. Finish: bay out. Chicken, rice, 430 g cream in; heat without boiling. Heat off. Taste; rest of the salt only if needed, pepper, then 36 g lemon juice last. Parsley on top.

If It’s Off

Weak on chickenMore Better Than Bouillon, before more salt
