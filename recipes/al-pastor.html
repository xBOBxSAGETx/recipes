Prep · Freeze · Griddle

Make Ahead & Cook

Prep & Freeze build the bags ahead — marinate happens in the freezer

  1. Toast chiles: dry skillet, medium. Guajillo, ancho, árbol 30–60 sec/side until fragrant and pliable — don’t burn (burnt = bitter adobo).
  2. Rehydrate: cover with hot water, soak 20–30 min until fully soft. Drain, reserving a little soaking liquid.
  3. Blend adobo: chiles + achiote + OJ + vinegar + garlic + spices + salt to smooth. Loosen with soaking liquid 15 ml at a time — it should coat a spoon, not run watery.
  4. Semi-freeze the pork: whole butt in the freezer 45–60 min until firm but not solid — this is what makes clean slicing possible.
  5. Slice: 3–4 mm against the grain. Thin is critical; work fast before the pork softens.
  6. Combine: toss the slices in the adobo until fully coated; massage it in.
  7. Portion + freeze: into quart bags (450 g each), press flat, remove air, label with the date, freeze.

Cook from Frozen ripping-hot griddle — sear, don’t steam

  1. Thaw: fridge overnight, or a cold-water bath 30–45 min.
  2. Griddle: cast iron / flat-top, high heat, light oil. Single layer, don’t crowd. Char 60–90 sec, flip once, char the other side.
  3. Chop + onion: push pork aside, add fresh sliced white onion; char it while chopping the pork with metal spatulas, then mix together on the griddle.
  4. Serve: double-stacked corn tortillas, al pastor, charred onion, cilantro, fresh lime, flaky salt on top.
