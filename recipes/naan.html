Naan

Yogurt-leavened · thin & pliable · no yeast · restaurant-style
Yield
~16 naan
Active
~35 min
Rest
3–6 hr
Hydration
~68%

Ingredients stretch to 2–3 mm · cook on a dry pan at 450–500°F

Dough
All-purpose flour440 g
Yogurt full-fat212 g
Water room temp90 g
Neutral oil30 g
Sugar12 g
Salt6 g
Baking powder4 g
Finish
Butter or ghee melted, for brushing120 g
Cilantro choppedoptional
Garlic minced, into the butteroptional
Nigella seedsoptional

Double batch — ~16 naan to feed a big curry. Single: halve everything (~8 naan). Triple: ×1.5 this batch (~24).

Why no yeast: yeast makes thick, bready naan. Yogurt + baking powder gives the thin, pliable texture with just enough lift for bubbles. Hand-stretch, never roll — rolling compresses the dough into a tough result; passing the ball between oiled palms and letting gravity do the work keeps it tender. Rest is flavor + workability: 3 hr minimum, 6 hr better; overnight in the fridge works (pull 1 hr before cooking).

Method · Pan · Char

Mix, Stretch & Char

Method screaming-hot dry cast iron, lid on first side, broiler char to finish

  1. Dry: whisk flour, baking powder, sugar, salt in a large bowl.
  2. Wet: add yogurt, water, oil; mix with a spatula to a shaggy mass.
  3. Knead: 5–7 min to a soft, tacky dough (tackier at this hydration) — wet or oiled hands, don’t add flour. The extra water is what keeps it soft as it cools.
  4. Rest: oiled bowl, covered, 3–6 hr at room temp (overnight in the fridge is fine).
  5. Divide: turn out onto a lightly oiled surface, divide into ~16 balls (~50 g).
  6. Stretch: oil your hands, press flat and hand-stretch by passing between palms to 2–3 mm, ~6–7″. Don’t roll.
  7. Heat: cast iron screaming hot, no oil — IR 450–500°F. Have a lid ready; preheat the broiler on high.
  8. Cook: lay the naan in the dry pan and immediately cover. 60–90 sec until bubbles form and the bottom has dark spots.
  9. Flip: uncovered 60–90 sec; press the edges with a spatula if needed.
  10. Char: transfer to a sheet, under the broiler 2–4″ from the element — watch closely, 10–20 sec for char.
  11. Finish: brush immediately with melted butter/ghee; stack in a towel-lined container to keep warm.

Troubleshooting garlic naan: stir minced garlic into the finishing butter
